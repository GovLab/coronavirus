Strategies

Strategies for implementing recommendations proposed by experts through Smarter Crowdsourcing conferences



The outcomes of this project are a set of 18 recommendations developed with the support of global experts. These recommendations are designed for government officials to aid them as they address the COVID-19 pandemic. Each recommendation is supported by a set of strategiesto achieve that recommendation. Strategies in turn detail actions and global examples that guide governments of the specific steps needed for implementing the strategy. Each strategy is a means to achieve a specific recommendation. You can explore individual strategies by searching by keywords, audience, type of strategy, and timeline.
