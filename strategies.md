# Strategies

Strategies for implementing recommendations proposed by experts through Smarter Crowdsourcing conferences

The outcomes of this project are a set of [18 recommendations](http://localhost:8000/recommendations.html) developed with the support of global experts. These recommendations are designed for government officials to aid them as they address the COVID-19 pandemic. Each recommendation is supported by [a set of strategies](http://localhost:8000/strategies.html)to achieve that recommendation. Strategies in turn detail actions and global examples that guide governments of the specific steps needed for implementing the strategy. Each strategy is a means to achieve a specific recommendation. You can explore individual strategies by searching by keywords, audience, type of strategy, and timeline.
