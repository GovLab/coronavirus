# Recommendations

Recommendations for governments to strengthen government capacity for COVID testing strategies, contact tracing programs, public health communications, vulnerable population supports, monitoring systems, and mental health interventions

The outcomes of this project are a set of [18 recommendations](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations) developed with the support of global experts. These recommendations are designed for government officials to aid them as they address the COVID-19 pandemic. Each recommendation is supported by [a set of strategies](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/strategies) to achieve that recommendation. Strategies in turn detail actions and global examples that guide governments of the specific steps needed for implementing the strategy.

[EXPLORE THE RECOMMENDATIONS](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations#recco) [EXPLORE THE STRATEGIES](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/strategies)

## Recommendations

[

Pooled Testing

Governments and researchers should implement pooled testing strategies, innovating with pool design through ranking schemes or geographic schemes.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/pooled-testing)

[

National Patient Identifiers

Governments should take steps toward developing a unique health identifier/national patient identifier system (UHI/NPI) that unambiguously identifies each individual and links her to her health records, including test results.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/national-patient-identifiers)

[

High-risk populations

Governments should prioritize conducting tests among populations with a high probability of 1) contracting the virus; 2), spreading it to others; and 3) suffering serious consequences as a result of infection.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/high-risk-populations)

[

Metrics

Governments should establish and use metrics to (1) indicate thresholds where contact tracing is more or less effective as a containment and mitigation strategy; and (2) track progress and effectiveness of ongoing contact tracing programs.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/metrics)

[

Interjurisdictional Coordination

Governments should increase vertical and horizontal interjurisdictional coordination by (1) incentivizing the use of interoperable or the same contact tracing platform and data collection process and (2) creating opportunities for knowledge sharing.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/interjurisdictional-coordination)

[

Localization

Governments should increase public trust in contact tracing programs by ensuring the programs incorporate local knowledge and experience. Specifically, they should (1) conduct an assessment that identifies community assets; (2) partner with local community organizations to identify contact tracers; (3) conduct a public health messaging campaign to explain contact tracing; and (4) incorporate information about the local community when training the contact tracing workforce.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/localization)

[

Improve Data Management

Improve data management (collection, storage, and analysis) processes to support evidence-driven programs and policies that identify vulnerable populations and account for their needs.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/improve-data-management)

[

Establish Public-Private Partnerships

Establish Public-Private Partnerships that support vulnerable populations by filling service delivery gaps that community-based organizations, governments, and NGOs struggle to execute efficiently working on their own, while also benefiting the private institutions by providing them with financing and other resources.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/establish-public-private-partnerships)

[

Engage the Public and Civil Society Organizations

Engage the public and civil society organizations to support vulnerable populations by capitalizing on existing knowledge and expertise, leveraging local assets and resources, building trust with intended beneficiaries, and strengthening the capacity of communities.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/engage-the-public-and-civil-society-organizations)

[

Enhance Data Collection

Enhance data collection capabilities through innovations in technologies, crowdsourcing, symptom-trackers, and partnerships.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/enhance-data-collection)

[

Improve Information Infrastructure

Improve public health monitoring and surveillance information infrastructure by standardizing data formats and definitions, sharing data, and developing regulations and legislation that support public health monitoring and surveillance.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/improve-information-infrastructure)

[

Increase Government Capacity

Increase government capacity to use public health monitoring and surveillance information to make policy and programmatic decisions.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/increase-government-capacity)

[

Government capacity and regulatory environment

Improve legislative and regulatory frameworks for mental health services.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/government-capacity-and-regulatory-environment)

[

Data and Technology

Use digital technologies and data to and expand access to mental health services and improve service delivery.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/data-and-technology)

[

Increase provider capacity

Increase provider capacity through innovations in training opportunities and strategies to expand the workforce supply.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/increase-provider-capacity)

[

Evidence and misinformation

Combat misinformation through evidence-based, clear, and consistent messaging.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/evidence-and-misinformation)

[

Community and stakeholder engagement

Engage communities and other stakeholders to make messaging strategies context specific and culturally relevant.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/community-and-stakeholder-engagement)

[

Data and innovation

Use data and innovations like crowdsourcing to optimize messaging development, reach, and uptake.

](https://coronavirus.smartercrowdsourcing.org/recommendations/data-and-innovation)
