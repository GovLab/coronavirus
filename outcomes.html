The outcomes of this project are a set of 18 recommendations developed with the support of global experts. These recommendations are designed for government officials to aid them as they address the COVID-19 pandemic. Each recommendation is supported by a set of strategies to achieve that recommendation. Strategies in turn detail actions and global examples that guide governments of the specific steps needed for implementing the strategy.

About the products

To view these resources you can either (1) download the master report (here) which has all of the materials compiled into a single resource or (2) visit the advisory session page (here) where you can view resources grouped by the respective session topic.

Conference Materials

Problem Catalogue

Problem identification and prioritization is the first phase of the Smarter Crowdsourcing process. This problem catalogue document is a catalogue of possible core problems related to the pandemic complete with brief descriptions of each problem. The IDB used this collection of problems to select the six priority problems that became the six sessions of the Smarter Crowdsourcing project. Each selected problem was the subject of an online advisory session to identify good practices worldwide and distill significant learnings that can help to better understand how these initiatives can be reproduced in the region.

  • Orientation to a scope of problems governments must address during the pandemic
  • Menu that allows government to select specific problem and target resources


Conference Materials

Problem Brief

These problem brief documents provide a short description of the problem that each session sets out to solve. In addition, they include a rundown of root causes, an explanation of why the problem is important and compelling to solve, sample solutions and framing questions for the online advisory session. 

  • Background information on problem
  • Framing questions for solution-oriented discussion


Conference Materials

Regional Report


These regional reports, one for each of the six advisory sessions,  contextualize the problem to the reality and situation in the partner and participating countries. The information is based on research as well as interviews with government representatives. The document highlights the concerns and specific questions that countries wanted answered/addressed.

  • Country-specific contextualization of the identified problem
  • Background information on country healthcare systems, public health infrastructure, and cultural context


Conference Materials

Participant Bios

For each online advisory session, the GovLab and the IDB identified and invited participants from diverse backgrounds who have the practical experience, subject matter and related data, tech or methods expertise and, above all, people who have demonstrated success solving the problem in the domain or a related domain. These participant biography documents list the biographies of those who participated in the advisory session. 

  • Menu of experts to consult for information and advice on potential solutions
  • Introduction to network of researchers, practitioners, government officials, and private sector innovators


Conference Materials

Session Takeaways

Each session takeaway document is a summary of solutions proposed during each online advisory session. It is not a transcript. This document is used to invite participating governments to select which solutions are of greatest use to them and then develop those ideas into actionable proposals. 

  • Cursory list of innovative ideas to explore and develop further
  • Foundation of recommendations and strategies in each recommendations memo


Conference Materials

Recommendations Memo

These recommendation memos are designed for government officials and civil servants who are interested in addressing the problem identified in the advisory session. Each document puts forth three recommendations on how to address the problem with accompanying implementation plans, examples, and experts to contact. The memos also incorporate considerations for governments trying to implement the recommendations as well as background information on the problem itself.

  • Detailed recommendations and action plans directed at government officials
  • List of examples, best practices, and experts to contact for further consultation


Final product

Executive Brief

These high-level briefing documents are designed for senior officials who do not need the level of detail in the recommendation memo. There is one brief for each of the six advisory sessions. Each briefing document contains a description of the topic/problem, recommendations for how to address the problem, strategies for implementing the recommendation, and examples of the strategies in action. The documents also contain resource estimates for the time and cost it may take to achieve  the recommendation as well as the estimated impact it will have if the recommendation is achieved.

  • For individuals who need top-level recommendations but don’t need implementation details
  • Complements recommendations memo as an executive summary.


Final product

Master Report

This 300+ page master report includes a synthesized compilation of all the resources from the Smarter Crowdsouricng in the Age of Coronavirus Project. The document is broken into six chapters - one for each topic covered during the engagement: Testing Strategies, Contact Tracing, Behavioral Science, Vulnerable Populations, Monitoring and Surveillance, and Mental Health and Emotional Wellbeing. This document is available in English, Spanish, and Portugese.

  • Resource manual consolidating all outputs from the project into a single document
  • Detailed implementation plans for implementing partners/stakeholders
